# TSH — "THE OTHER ROBIN"
## Cold open: script and shot list (draft for review — nothing here is animated yet)

**Runtime:** about 3 to 4 minutes, plus the guitar game (1.5 minutes).
**Format:** letterboxed 2.39:1 for every film moment; the bars slide away only when the player has control.
**Tone:** two halves that rhyme. The city is cold, blue and official. Her room is warm, loud and messy. The phone call
is the hinge where the cold leaks in.

> Everything in *italics* is a suggestion to react to. Dialogue is written to be spoken; change anything.

---

## SCENE 1 — EXT. NEW YORK CITY — NIGHT

*Year title only. No location card yet; the city should feel like it could be anywhere it has gone wrong.*

**FADE IN on black.** A low synth drone. Rain on glass. Far off, a siren that never quite arrives.

> **THE DIRECTOR (V.O.)**
> *(calm, practised; she has given this speech many times)*
> Ten years ago, the weapons got small enough to wear.

The city, from very high: a grid of light under cloud. Towers lit from inside. Rain.

> **THE DIRECTOR (V.O.)**
> A ring. A bracelet. A pair of shoes.

Street level. Neon Avenue. Three figures under a broken sign. Something on one of their wrists *glows*.

> **THE DIRECTOR (V.O.)**
> The gangs wore them first.

A flash. A car alarm. A shop window shatters into the street in slow motion.

> **THE DIRECTOR (V.O.)**
> Our streets became battlegrounds. Our children grew up afraid.

Red and blue. WFC vans slide in across the avenue. Officers in grey armour step out in a line, visors down. A drone
drops into frame, its red eye opening.

> **THE DIRECTOR (V.O.)**
> The World Field Command is here to take this city back.

The gang scatters. One of them is too slow. The drone's light finds him.

> **THE DIRECTOR (V.O.)**
> To make New York safe again.

Push in on the WFC billboard, forty feet high over the avenue: **WFC — PROTECTING NEW YORK.** The Director's face on
it, half-lit, looking down at the street. *We don't learn who she is to Robin yet; that lands later.*

**TITLE:** NEW YORK CITY · 2044

**SMASH CUT TO:** a guitar riff, full volume.

## SCENE 2 — INT. ROBIN'S ROOM — NIGHT (21:58)

*Every shot is cut to the song. The camera behaves like the music: handheld, energetic, close.*

The song hits. A speaker on the floor, its light bars jumping.

ROBIN RYU (16) bursts through the frame at a sprint, from the kitchen, across the flat, and **launches onto her bed**.
She bounces on the beat. Long black hair everywhere. Gray T-shirt, baggy jeans, socks.

She is singing along, badly and loudly, eyes closed.

Her room in fragments, cut to the beat:
- A workbench: sewing machine, circuit boards, a soldering iron still warm.
- A dress form wearing a black jacket with light seams running down the sleeves.
- A table of shipping boxes, labelled, taped. A burner phone. Cash under a mug.
- White sneakers by the window, coils visible in the soles.
- A note on the kitchen table she walks straight past.

She spins in the middle of the room. Slides across the floor in her socks. Air-drums on the bench.

Then she stops. Sees the RED GUITAR leaning on the amp. A beat of decision. *A grin.*

She grabs it, throws the strap over her shoulder, kicks the amp on. Feedback screams.

**THE GAME BEGINS.**

## SCENE 3 — INT. ROBIN'S ROOM — CONTINUOUS

The last chord rings out. Robin falls backwards onto the bed, guitar on her chest, out of breath, laughing.

Her phone buzzes against the mattress. **CANON.**

She answers without looking, still lying down.

> **ROBIN**
> Mm.

> **CANON (V.O.)**
> *(on the phone)*
> You alive? You missed chem. Again.

> **ROBIN**
> I was there. Spiritually.

She sits up. Gets up. Paces, as she always does on the phone. Past the bench, the boxes, the window.

> **CANON (V.O.)**
> Teacher says one more missed lab write-up and she's calling home.

> **ROBIN**
> *(a glance at Mom's closed door)*
> She can try. Nobody's ever home.

*A beat. That one cost her something.*

> **CANON (V.O.)**
> You're coming tomorrow, though. I'm not building that robot by myself.

> **ROBIN**
> Yes, Canon. I'll be there.

> **CANON (V.O.)**
> On time?

> **ROBIN**
> *(smiling)*
> Don't push it.

The phone buzzes against her ear. A second call. She pulls it away and looks: **UNKNOWN NUMBER.**

The smile goes. She looks at the boxes on the table.

> **ROBIN**
> Hold on. Let me call you back. I have to do something.

> **CANON (V.O.)**
> Robin, wait—

She switches calls. Her voice changes: lower, flatter, business.

> **THE BUYER (V.O.)**
> Hey. You got my order?

She picks up a small box from the table. Two rings inside.

> **ROBIN**
> Yeah. It's ready.

She hangs up. Looks at the window. Looks at the sneakers. Then at the jacket on the dress form.

**CUT TO:** the existing night begins: the kit, the window, Kiln Street.

## Production notes

- **Her mother:** the Director is Robin's mother. Scene 1 shows her face on the billboard, Scene 3 shows her absence (the
  closed door, "nobody's ever home"). The connection is never said. Do you want it said here, or saved for later?
- **The phone:** Mixamo's phone clips cover Scene 3 (standing, pacing). For 3A she needs a lying-down answer; the
  closest existing pose is sitting up.
- **What doesn't exist yet:** the kid at the bus stop (1F), the shop window shattering (1E), the string snapping, the
  ring box, the guitar strap. Each would need building.
- **Voice:** subtitles only for now, as asked.
- **Music:** the song is the one composed for the game; a real track can replace it.
