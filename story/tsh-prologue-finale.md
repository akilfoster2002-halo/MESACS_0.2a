# TSH — Prologue Finale: "Under the Billboard"

**Picks up:** right after the Dragon Alley fight ("Send me an invoice.")
**Ends:** the prologue. Robin is asleep at 3:07 AM, and Maya and Kai are watching her through binoculars.

**Flow:** billboard → WFC ambush → street chase (fight on the move) → wall climb → super jump with drones firing → hiding tutorial → Mom calls → second half of the chase (everything together) → home → bedroom cutscene → Maya and Kai.

**Controls used (same keys as the rest of TSH):**
WASD move · Shift run / slide · Space jump, and hold Space for the Skyline shoes' super jump · G Gecko cuffs (climb) · E use / hide · F flash bangles · J static studs · Click punch · Right-click parry.

## 1. CUTSCENE — THE BILLBOARD

EXT. DRAGON ALLEY MOUTH — NIGHT

The last thug is face-down in a puddle. The buyer limps off into the dark.

Robin pockets her phone, slings her bag over her shoulder and walks out of the alley.

The street opens up, wet and neon-lit. Across from her, a digital billboard covers the side of a whole building.

**BILLBOARD:**
> **WFC**
> PROTECTING NEW YORK.
> SECURITY. ORDER. TRUST.

WFC officers stand in a heroic line over the skyline, with drones flying in formation behind them.

Robin stops and looks up at it.

**ROBIN:**
"Yeah. Sure."

The billboard flickers. The ad cuts out and the screen goes red.

**BILLBOARD:**
> ⚠ CITIZEN ALERT ⚠
> ILLEGAL WEARABLE ACTIVITY REPORTED
> SECTOR 9 — DRAGON ALLEY

Robin reads it and checks the street sign above her head: **DRAGON ALLEY.**

**ROBIN:**
"...Huh."

Red light washes over her. A drone drops out of the sky and stops at eye level, its scanner beam right on her gloves.

**DRONE (synthetic voice):**
"UNREGISTERED WEARABLE DETECTED."

Black WFC vehicles skid in from both ends of the street. Doors fly open and officers pour out with their rifles up.

**WFC OFFICER:**
"THERE! DROP THE BAG! GET ON THE GROUND!"

Robin looks up at the billboard, then at the officers.

**ROBIN:**
"Okay, little weird."

The officers advance. Robin slowly raises her hands, then lowers them.

**ROBIN:**
"Actually..."

She runs.

**★ HEAT: 3 STARS** (the screen tints, the music hits)

## 2. GAMEPLAY — THE STREET (fight on the move)

**OBJECTIVE: LOSE THE WFC**

This section teaches that fighting during a chase isn't about winning fights. It's about clearing a path.

### 2a. Ground navigation

The street is a straight line packed with obstacles: parked cars, a food cart, concrete barriers and a scaffold.

| Prompt | What the player does |
|---|---|
| **SHIFT — SPRINT** | Robin breaks away from the first squad. |
| **SPACE at a barrier — VAULT** | She clears a concrete barrier. Officers behind her have to go around it. |
| **SHIFT while sprinting — SLIDE** | She slides under the scaffold pipe and comes up running. |
| **SPACE onto a car** | Hood, roof, trunk. She runs across the cars, and the alarms go off one after another. |

**WFC OFFICER (behind her):**
"STOP!"

**ROBIN (looking back while running):**
"You're really committed to this!"

### 2b. The cutoff (first fight in a chase)

A WFC van brakes sideways ahead of her. Two officers jump out with stun batons. **The game drops into a quick fight.** There's no arena and no lock-in, so the street keeps moving around her.

| Prompt | What the player does |
|---|---|
| **RIGHT-CLICK — PARRY** | The first baton swing is slowed down for the lesson. She catches it with her glove. |
| **CLICK — COUNTER** | Officer one goes down. |
| **G — PULL** | Officer two is reaching for his radio. She yanks him off his feet and into the side of the van. |

**ROBIN:**
"Sorry! Reflex!"

A **SLOW-MO PROMPT** appears: **"Don't stay. More are coming."** If the player keeps fighting, the squad behind her catches up and her heat goes up. The lesson is that you hit, then go.

### 2c. First drone strike

A drone swings into the street ahead of her. Its beam turns from **cyan to red.**

**DRONE:**
"NON-COMPLIANCE CONFIRMED. ENGAGING."

**TUTORIAL: INCOMING**
A red line traces from the drone to the ground in front of Robin. A lock-on tone beeps, faster and faster. A ring of red light marks where it will hit.

**"GET OUT OF THE RING."**

The drone fires a **micro-missile.** The player sidesteps (or slides) out of the ring.

BOOM. A parked car flips onto its side behind her.

**ROBIN:**
"Are you KIDDING me?!"

**How the missiles work (rules the whole chase follows):**
- Every missile is **telegraphed:** a red line, a lock-on tone, then an impact ring about 1.5 seconds before it lands.
- A missile hits **the ring, not Robin.** Moving always saves her.
- Missiles **tear up the street.** Cars flip, scaffolds drop and fire hydrants burst. Sometimes the wreck becomes a new vault or a new cover spot.
- A hit doesn't kill her outright. It knocks her down, and while she gets up, officers close in. Two knockdowns before she breaks away means she's caught (see Fail State).

### 2d. Dead end

The street funnels into an alley. A WFC barricade slams down across the far end.

There's a dead end ahead and boots behind her. Robin looks up and sees a brick wall, five stories high, with no fire escape.

**ROBIN:**
"Okay. Okay okay okay."

## 3. GAMEPLAY — THE WALL (Gecko cuffs)

**TUTORIAL: G — GECKO CUFFS**

Robin jumps at the wall and her gloves light up green. Her hands stick to the brick.

**ROBIN:**
"Please work."

They hold.

| Prompt | What the player does |
|---|---|
| **G at a wall — GRAB** | She sticks to the wall. |
| **W / A / S / D — CLIMB** | She climbs up and across, around windows and AC units. |
| **SPACE on the wall — LEAP UP** | She jumps a whole floor. It's faster, but the cuffs flash bright when she does. |

Officers flood into the alley below.

**WFC OFFICER:**
"She's on the building!"

**ROBIN (looking down):**
"Yeah. I noticed."

**DRONE PRESSURE:** A drone rises up alongside the wall and lines up a shot. Its lock-on ring appears **on the wall itself.** The player has to climb **sideways** out of it. The missile blows out a window where she was, and glass rains down on the officers below.

**SECONDARY LESSON:** One officer below has a **spotlight rifle.** While its beam is on her, **her climbing speed is halved.** Climbing around a drainpipe or an AC unit breaks the line of sight. This is the first hint that **being seen is the real danger.**

Robin hauls herself over the edge of the roof and rolls onto gravel.

**ROBIN (panting):**
"Okay. That's new."

## 4. GAMEPLAY — THE ROOFTOPS (super jump under fire)

**TUTORIAL: HOLD SPACE — SKYLINE SHOES**

The next roof is across a six-lane avenue, much too far for a normal jump.

**ROBIN:**
"Okay, Robin. Don't embarrass yourself."

| Prompt | What the player does |
|---|---|
| **HOLD SPACE — CHARGE** | The shoes hum and the coils glow. An arc shows where she'll land. |
| **RELEASE — BOUND** | BOOM. She launches into the air and the whole city opens up beneath her. |
| **MOUSE in the air — STEER** | She drifts toward the rooftop she's aiming for. |
| **SHIFT in the air — DIVE** | She drops fast. |
| **SPACE as she lands, on the gold ring — PERFECT LANDING** | She rolls and keeps her speed. |

She lands, rolls and stands up.

**ROBIN:**
"Okay. Definitely keeping that."

### 4a. Drones in the sky

Now the drones get serious. **Three of them** come up over the edges of the roofs and pace her from the air.

The player learns that **the air is the most dangerous place to be.**
- While Robin is mid-jump, a drone can fire a **lock-on missile that follows her.** The tone screams.
- **Dodge it with a DIVE (Shift) or by changing direction mid-air.** The missile overshoots and blows apart a water tower behind her.
- **Better plan:** don't jump while a drone has a clear line on her. Wait behind a chimney, then go.

### 4b. Rooftop officers

A WFC rappel team drops onto the roof ahead of her from a hovering transport.

The player chooses what to do:
- **Fight through:** parry the first one, pull the second, and keep moving.
- **Go around:** cuff up the side of a stairwell hut and jump over them.
- **F — FLASH BANGLES (tutorial):** a burst of white light blinds every officer and drone near her for a few seconds. **ROBIN:** "Smile!"

### 4c. The gate

A rooftop security gate starts rolling shut between two buildings.

**PROMPT: SHIFT — SLIDE**

She slides under it with an inch to spare. The officers behind her slam into the bars.

**ROBIN:**
"Seriously?!"

## 5. GAMEPLAY — HIDE (the hiding tutorial)

A **WFC gunship** heaves up over the skyline. It's huge and loud. **A searchlight** the width of a house sweeps across the rooftops.

**GUNSHIP PA:**
"SUSPECT IS ON THE ROOFTOPS. ALL UNITS, SECTOR 9."

There's nowhere to run. The next roof is too far, the gunship is between her and the gap, and the searchlight is coming her way.

Robin spots a big rooftop AC unit with a gap behind it.

**TUTORIAL: E — HIDE**
**"You can't outrun everything. When you can't run, disappear."**

Robin dives behind the AC unit. The camera tightens. The music drops to a low pulse with the thump of the rotors over it.

**How hiding works:**
- **Cover spots** (AC units, water tanks, vents, stairwell huts and dumpsters on the street) glow faintly when she's near one. Press **E** to tuck in.
- While she's hidden, **WASD shuffles her around the cover.** This is the game. She keeps the box between herself and whatever is looking.
- An **eye icon** above each officer and drone fills up when they're getting close to seeing her. If it fills all the way, she's spotted.
- **Searchlights** sweep in patterns the player can read. She moves when the light moves away.
- **Drone scanner beams** can see behind some cover. **J — STATIC STUDS (tutorial):** a burst from her earrings fills every drone nearby with static. They're blind for six seconds, and so is their beam. **ROBIN (whispering):** "Shhh."
- **Hiding is not failing.** Heat stars **cool down only while nobody can see her.** Every second hidden is a second closer to escaping.

An officer climbs out of the stairwell hut and walks the roof with his rifle light on. The player shuffles Robin around the AC unit, staying opposite him. He passes. The searchlight sweeps over the top of the unit, and she stays still.

The officer stops and listens. Then he walks back toward the stairwell.

Robin exhales.

**★★★ → ★★** (one star cools off)

## 6. GAMEPLAY / CUTSCENE — MOM CALLS

Her phone **rings.** It's loud.

Robin freezes and grabs it to muffle it. She looks at the screen: **MOM.**

**ROBIN (whispering):**
"Oh, you've gotta be kidding me."

The officer at the stairwell **stops.** His eye icon starts filling.

**PROMPT: E — ANSWER** (the ringing is louder than her voice)

The game doesn't cut away. **The call plays while the player is still hidden.** Robin keeps whispering, and the player still has to **shuffle around the AC unit** because the officer has started walking back toward the sound.

**ROBIN:**
"Hey, Mom."

**MOM:**
"Hey, Robin. I'm still at work. I think I'm going to be later than usual tonight."

(The officer comes around the left side, so the player shuffles right.)

**ROBIN:**
"Okay. Yeah. No problem."

**MOM:**
"You okay? You sound out of breath."

**ROBIN:**
"Yeah. I'm fine. Just... stairs."

A drone **fires at something two roofs over.** BOOM. The sirens climb.

**MOM:**
"What was that?"

**ROBIN:**
"Uh... TV."

**MOM:**
"At this hour?"

The searchlight crawls up the AC unit and stops an inch from her shoe. Robin pulls her foot in.

**ROBIN:**
"Really good TV."

Her mom laughs.

**MOM:**
"Alright. Don't stay up too late."

Robin looks at the phone, then up at the WFC gunship hanging over her head with its logo lit up on the hull.

**ROBIN (quietly, guilty):**
"Yeah. You too."

She hangs up. Beat.

**ROBIN:**
"...Crap."

A shadow falls over her. The officer is standing **right there.** He reaches for his radio.

**ROBIN:**
"Oh, come on."

**PROMPT: CLICK — TAKEDOWN** (one slow-motion punch) **or SPACE — BOLT**

Either way she's out of cover and running. The gunship's searchlight snaps onto her.

**★★ → ★★★★** (the gunship has her. Four stars.)

## 7. GAMEPLAY — THE SECOND HALF (everything at once)

**OBJECTIVE: GET HOME** (a marker appears on her apartment building, eight blocks away)

There are no more prompts. The player now has to **run → fight → climb → jump → hide → repeat** under real pressure. The route is open, but every path mixes the same pieces:

1. **The gunship strafe.** The gunship marks a **line** of impact rings across a rooftop, and they go off one after another. The player runs **sideways** across the line, not along it, or slides under a billboard frame to get out of its sight.
2. **The billboard wall.** She climbs the frame of a giant WFC billboard **with the cuffs, straight across the face of the ad.** The WFC slogan glows huge under her hands: **PROTECTING NEW YORK.** A drone lines up a missile, and the player climbs out of the ring. The missile blows a hole through the screen, and sparks rain into the street. *(Optional line, ROBIN: "Sorry! Not sorry.")*
3. **The two-drone jump.** It's a long super jump across an alley with two drones crossing the gap. The player has to time the launch **between their beams,** dive under the first missile and steer around the second.
4. **The rooftop squad.** Six officers spread across a roof. It's the biggest fight of the chase, but the right answer is **flash (F), pull one into the others, slide under the gate, gone.** Players who stay and brawl are allowed to, but reinforcements keep dropping from the transport.
5. **The last hide.** A water tower roof. The player **jams the drones (J)** and ducks behind the tank. Then **another full WFC squad runs right past her,** close enough to touch, and keeps going.

**Hidden. Nobody can see her. ★★★★ → ★★★ → ★★ → ★ → gone.**

The sirens fade. The gunship banks away toward the river.

**THE LAST JUMP**

Her building is across a gap that looks too big, far bigger than any jump so far. Robin looks at the gap, then at her shoes.

**ROBIN:**
"Okay, Robin."

She takes a breath.

**ROBIN:**
"Don't embarrass yourself."

**HOLD SPACE.** The charge goes past full. The coils whine and turn white-hot. **RELEASE.**

The biggest launch of the night. The city drops away, and for one second there's no music and no sirens. Just wind, and New York lit up beneath her.

She **barely** catches the edge of her own roof. She scrambles up and rolls onto her back. Silence.

**ROBIN (staring at the sky):**
"Okay..."

She sits up and looks back across the city. Far off, red and blue lights are still searching the wrong neighborhood.

**ROBIN:**
"Definitely never doing that again."

Beat.

**ROBIN:**
"...Probably."

She pulls out her phone. The screen lights her face.

**3:07 AM**

**ROBIN:**

On the floor, the shoes' glow slowly fades out.

## Design notes

**What each part teaches**

| Section | New skill | Threat |
|---|---|---|
| 2. The street | sprint, vault, slide, parry and pull while moving | officers, the first missile |
| 3. The wall | Gecko cuffs climbing, leap up | spotlight rifle, a missile on the wall |
| 4. Rooftops | super jump, steering, dive, perfect landing, flash | drones in the air, homing missiles, rappel teams |
| 5. Hide | cover, shuffling around it, reading searchlights, jamming drones | gunship searchlight, patrolling officer |
| 6. Mom's call | hiding while something else is going on | the officer coming back toward the sound |
| 7. Second half | everything together, no prompts | gunship strafing runs, two drones at once, a squad of six |

**Fail state.** Being caught (two missile knockdowns, or tackled by an officer while down) cuts to Robin cuffed and face-down, then **restarts from the last checkpoint.** Checkpoints are at the start of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7 (and halfway through 7). It's never a game over.

**Fairness rules for the drones.** Every missile is telegraphed (a red line, a lock-on tone, an impact ring). There are never more than two missiles in the air at once. The gunship strafe always leaves a gap. Drones don't fire while Robin is hidden; they search instead.

**Pacing.** The music builds for the whole chase. It drops to near silence for the hide and the phone call, crashes back in when the officer finds her, and cuts out completely during the final jump.

**The theme beat.** Robin hides from the WFC while lying to her mom on the phone, with the WFC gunship hanging over her. That's the first time the player feels the two halves of her life collide. Later, the bedroom shows the cost (3 AM, school in the morning), and the binoculars show the hook (someone has noticed her).

## In the game

This finale is built: `public/tshchase.js` (the chase), with the films and the ending in `public/tsh.js` and the rules in `public/tshai.js` (`CHASE`). It replaces what used to come after the Dragon Alley fight. The old scenes (the news on the screens, Maya's roof, the flat) are kept in the code for Part Two.

Where the build differs from the script above:
- **The shoes are cold after the fight.** In this game Robin already learned the shoes on the way to the alley, so the chase keeps them off (on foot, vaulting, climbing) until the roofs. Then the *HOLD SPACE* card says they're charged again.
- **The billboard runs WFC's ad first.** Robin says "Yeah. Sure." to it, then it cuts to the alert for Dragon Alley, where she's standing.
- **The wall is a WFC barrier dropping across the gap between two buildings** (there's no fire escape there), climbed with the Gecko cuffs.
- **The gate is on a rooftop.** You can slide under it, or bound over the fence.
- **There's no single scripted "biggest jump".** The last stretch is open: any route home works, as long as she lands on her own roof with no stars.
- **The new lines play as subtitles only** until they're recorded (`node tools/tsh-voices.mjs todo` lists them). Mom on the phone uses the Director's voice, since they're the same woman.
